## Technical meaning of the demonstration

The central design issue is **mould release**. A mould must open along directions that allow the part to leave the steel without any portion of the part projecting behind or beneath a mould surface. The speaker first considers the Z-axis. Because the nozzle has a circumferential groove and projecting flange-like regions, a Z-axis draw would cause the groove-related faces to act as undercuts.

The speaker therefore chooses the Y-axis as the principal moulding direction. A parting plane is placed so that the two main mould halves can separate along that direction. However, changing the main direction does not allow every feature to be formed by the two principal halves. The internal cylindrical wall/central pin-like passage requires a **side core**, which enters or withdraws laterally to form the internal wall and then release it.

The colours in the demonstration represent design roles rather than actual material specifications. The yellow/orange region represents one main mould half, the cyan region represents the opposing mould half, and the purple/dark-blue region represents the side core or pin-forming component. The green highlighting indicates selection or a steel/body region during the CAD operations, while the arrows are explanatory annotations: blue for the main moulding direction and red for the side-core/alternative movement direction.
